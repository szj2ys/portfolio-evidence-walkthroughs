# Portfolio evidence walkthroughs

Six original fictional artifact packets for practising a project or portfolio discussion. Each exercise includes concrete materials, contribution boundaries, a two-minute outline, skeptical follow-ups with reasoned answers, a fillable printable sheet, and explicit limitations.

Published by the MakeInterview/MockLive team. This is a manual practice resource; the separate live AI interview service is currently paused.

## Choose a packet

| Page | Inspectable materials | Main practice task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `code-change-and-tests.html` | Python helper, input/output cases, reviewer note | Explain a bounded change without claiming the whole system. |
| `research-synthesis.html` | Three fictional notes and a traceability table | Separate source statements, interpretation, and counterevidence. |
| `ux-design-revision.html` | Before/after interaction sketches and review log | Distinguish design intent from implemented or tested outcomes. |
| `operations-handoff-map.html` | Handoff maps and normal/exception traces | Name responsibility and avoid treating a diagram as adoption evidence. |
| `support-knowledge-article.html` | Article revision and scenario-review notes | Explain scope, safe next steps, and unresolved product behaviour. |
| `experiment-readout.html` | Event definition, six traces, and a withheld readout | Explain a data-quality concern without declaring an unsupported winner. |

All people, products, artifacts, data, review notes, and results are invented for teaching. None are real customer evidence or experience a candidate may claim. The exercises do not assess hiring suitability, establish product outcomes, or replace a real research, accessibility, security, or statistical review.

## Practical use

Open `index.html`, choose a packet, and inspect its evidence before reading the proposed follow-up answers. Rehearse the 120-second outline with a timer while pointing to the relevant artifact. Ask a peer to challenge one claim. Keep the answer tied to what the packet supports, including its gaps.

For your own portfolio, use true work you are authorised to disclose. Show what you personally did and where colleagues contributed. Label reproductions and fictional reconstructions clearly. Do not put confidential source material in a shared planning sheet or invent results to complete the story.

## Offline, temporary notes, and printing

Keep the seven HTML files and `style.css` together. Open the index in any current browser. No installation, account, runtime dependency, external JavaScript, analytics, or upload is needed. Only optional related reading links need the internet.

Type short notes in the labelled planning boxes or write on paper. The pages do not send or store notes; reload or close may discard them. Copy anything you want to retain. The browser Print command prints the packet and planning sheet. Keep typed notes inside the visible boxes. Code wraps visually on small screens; its underlying text remains unchanged when copied.
